## Design Validation

❌ **Low Cascade sensitivity**: 0/4 events detected. H2 proxy (OHLCV-based) may not separate Cascade from Normal states well. Wave 3 tick-level Hawkes required for better performance.

✅ **False positive rate**: 0/3 control events (0%) exceeded threshold.

**Baseline critical rate**: 4.2% of rolling 90-day windows have α/β > 0.85. In production, this rate should be elevated near Cascade events.

**Production recommendation per v2.1 §2.1**: H2 branching ratio is ONE of two new main conditions (alongside TDA1 L^1). Combined logic: (σ-based full + one of TDA/Hawkes) OR (σ-based partial + both TDA and Hawkes).

## Limitations

- **Proxy events**: Using |return| > 1σ as Hawkes event surrogate. True Hawkes requires trade-level LOB data (Wave 3).
- **4H discretisation**: Hawkes is a continuous-time model. Discrete 4H bins underestimate inter-event timing.
- **Cascade sample**: 4 events — too small for robust validation.
- **Regime stationarity**: BTC structure changed post-ETF approval (Jan 2024). Full-dataset fit may blend different regimes.
- **MLE sensitivity**: Hawkes NLL surface can be flat; Nelder-Mead with restarts may not converge to global optimum for all windows.
